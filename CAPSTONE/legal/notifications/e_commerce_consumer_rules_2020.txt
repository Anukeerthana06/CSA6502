CONSUMER PROTECTION (E-COMMERCE) RULES, 2020
MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION

Rule 4. Duties of e-commerce entities.
(1) Every e-commerce entity shall provide the following information in a clear and accessible manner on its platform:
(a) legal name of the e-commerce entity;
(b) principal geographic address of its headquarters and all branches;
(c) name and details of its website;
(d) contact details including e-mail address, fax, and customer care numbers for consumer grievance redressal.

Rule 5. Liabilities of marketplace e-commerce entities.
(1) A marketplace e-commerce entity shall ensure that the ticket number is provided for each complaint lodged by a consumer and the consumer can track the status of the complaint.
(2) Every e-commerce entity shall effect all refunds requested by the consumers according to the prescribed Reserve Bank of India timelines after the refund request has been accepted.
