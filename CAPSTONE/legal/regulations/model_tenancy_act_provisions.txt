MODEL TENANCY ACT & RENT CONTROL PRINCIPLES

CHAPTER II - TENANCY
Section 4. Tenancy Agreement.
(1) No person shall let or take on rent any premises except by an agreement in writing, which shall be informed to the Rent Authority.

Section 10. Protection against eviction.
(1) No landlord shall cut off or withhold any essential supply or service in the premises occupied by the tenant.
(2) In case of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months, the landlord may approach the Rent Court for recovery of possession.

Section 11. Security Deposit.
(1) The security deposit to be paid by the tenant in advance shall not exceed:
(a) two months' rent in case of residential premises;
(b) six months' rent in case of non-residential premises.
