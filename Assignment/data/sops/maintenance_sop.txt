Standard Operating Procedure: General Rotating Equipment Maintenance (Sample)

1. Purpose
This sample SOP defines the general steps for scheduled preventive
maintenance and fault inspection of rotating equipment (motors, pumps,
fans) in the IndustroSense AI demonstration knowledge base.

2. Scope
Applies to general-purpose induction motors and centrifugal pumps
covered by this sample knowledge base. It is not a substitute for
equipment-specific manufacturer instructions.

3. Pre-Maintenance Safety Steps
   3.1 Notify affected personnel and operations before starting work.
   3.2 De-energize the equipment at the disconnect and apply
       lockout/tagout (LOTO) per facility policy.
   3.3 Verify zero energy state before touching any rotating or
       electrical component.
   3.4 Allow hot components to cool to a safe touch temperature before
       inspection.
   3.5 Never remove guards, disable interlocks, or bypass emergency
       stop circuits to expedite maintenance.

4. Routine Inspection Checklist (Every 500 Operating Hours, Sample Interval)
   4.1 Visual inspection for discoloration, damage, or leaks.
   4.2 Vibration check using a handheld vibration meter; compare to
       baseline reading for the asset.
   4.3 Temperature check of housing/bearing surfaces.
   4.4 Lubrication check and top-up per lubrication schedule.
   4.5 Check mounting bolts and coupling alignment.
   4.6 Record all readings in the maintenance log.

5. When to Escalate to Qualified Personnel
   5.1 Any sign of electrical fault (burning smell, sparking, tripped
       breaker that will not reset).
   5.2 Vibration levels significantly above baseline.
   5.3 Repeated overheating after a single maintenance cycle.
   5.4 Any situation involving hazardous, pressurized, or energized
       systems that the on-site technician is not certified to handle.

6. Documentation
   6.1 Every maintenance or inspection action must be logged with
       timestamp, technician identifier, equipment ID, and findings.
   6.2 Deviations from the standard interval must be justified and
       recorded.

7. Notice
This is a simulated SOP created for a Generative AI capstone
demonstration. It does not represent the maintenance policy of any
real facility and must not be used for actual industrial operations.
