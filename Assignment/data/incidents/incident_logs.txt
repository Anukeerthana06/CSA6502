Incident Record A
Equipment: Induction motor, conveyor drive
Reported symptom: Motor housing hot to the touch after roughly 25
minutes of continuous run time; slight vibration noted at the
non-drive end bearing.
Finding: Bearing grease had degraded and partially dried out,
increasing friction and localized heat. Root cause was an extended
interval since the last lubrication service.
Corrective action: Bearing re-greased per lubrication schedule; motor
returned to normal operating temperature within one operating cycle
after service.

Incident Record B
Equipment: Centrifugal pump, cooling water circuit
Reported symptom: Rattling noise near the pump inlet and mild
vibration during startup, disappearing after a few minutes of
operation.
Finding: Intermittent cavitation caused by a partially closed suction
valve that had not been fully reopened after a prior isolation.
Corrective action: Suction valve fully opened; cavitation noise did
not recur on subsequent runs.

Incident Record C
Equipment: Induction motor, fan drive
Reported symptom: Motor tripped on thermal overload twice in one
week; no unusual vibration reported.
Finding: Phase imbalance measured at the motor terminals exceeded the
facility's sample threshold, traced to a loose terminal connection on
one phase.
Corrective action: Terminal connection cleaned and re-torqued to
specification; phase currents balanced on re-test.
